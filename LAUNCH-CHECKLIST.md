# Before launch: what’s still needed

## Information only you can provide

1. Domain name: www.herclosingedit.com (set in `site.json` and `admin/config.yml`)
2. Contact email address for the site
3. Legal business name, licence details and address for the policy pages
4. Your refund approach for digital products
5. Governing law for the terms
6. A founder photograph (portrait, at least 1200px tall)
7. Social media links
8. Editorial photography you have rights to use (your own, or licensed stock)

## Files to create

1. The free guide PDF: Your First Steps Into High-Ticket Closing
2. The First Closing Role Toolkit files
3. The Feminine Closing Playbook files

## Integrations to connect

1. Netlify form notifications to your email (works immediately, free)
2. An email marketing service to deliver the guide automatically, run the five-email sequence and handle unsubscribes. MailerLite and Kit both have free starter plans and connect to Netlify forms via Zapier or Make, or by using their own embed forms in place of the Netlify ones.
3. Stripe (or similar) for checkout, plus a way to deliver download links after purchase
4. Google Analytics 4
5. Google Search Console

## Pages hidden from Google until finished

Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions and Refund Policy show a visible notice and carry a noindex tag. Fill in every [TO CONFIRM] item, untick “Unfinished” in the admin, and ideally have them reviewed by a qualified adviser.

## Final checks

1. Submit each form once on the live site and confirm it appears in Netlify Forms
2. Test the guide email end to end before switching Guide status to Live
3. Check every page on your phone
4. Submit the sitemap in Search Console
